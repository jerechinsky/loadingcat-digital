A digital variant of "loading cat" by zbw / zbzbw. Really like the original, just wanted a digital clock too. Thanks to zbw for the watchface and cat artwork!

- stacked hours and minutes, with four fonts and 12/24-hour time
- optional temperature in °C or °F, using your phone's location or a chosen city, village or neighborhood
- type a place and tap a result, with city, region and country shown
- a seconds spinner with 6, 7, 8, 10 or 12 spokes
- wrist-flick and supported backlight spins, with two speeds and 2–4-second animations
- optional nighttime pause for animations; seconds keep going
- optional disconnect and reconnect vibrations, with separate patterns and a Quiet Time override
- optional white cat on a black background when disconnected
- dithered spinner shading and an optional gray nose on monochrome watches

Works on all Pebble models. Weather refreshes every 15, 30 or 60 minutes; turning it off stops location and weather requests.
