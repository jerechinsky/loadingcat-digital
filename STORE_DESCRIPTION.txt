a digital variant of "loading cat" by zbw / zbzbw. really like the original, just wanted a digital clock too. thanks to zbw for the watchface and cat artwork!

stacked time on the left, optional temperature on the right. the spinner follows the seconds, and you can flick your wrist to give it a spin. choose up to 12 spokes, a few fonts and animation options. you can pause flick and backlight spins at night while the seconds keep going. optional phone disconnect vibration, with four patterns and a Quiet Time override. works on all pebble models.
