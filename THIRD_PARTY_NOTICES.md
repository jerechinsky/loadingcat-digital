## Original numeral designs

**Square** and **Square Cut** were drawn specifically for this adaptation. Their vector outlines are generated by `tools/generate_square_fonts.py` from original geometric shapes; no third-party font outlines are used in these two families. Teko and Bebas Neue remain the other two menu choices. Other licensed font sources above are retained from earlier studies and are no longer selectable.
