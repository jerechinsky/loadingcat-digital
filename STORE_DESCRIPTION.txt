A digital take on Loading Cat, with stacked time, optional local temperature and a white-to-gray spinner that follows the seconds. Flick your wrist to give it a spin that gently slows down.

Choose from four fonts, slow or fast spins, 12/24-hour time and °C/°F. Weather and animation can be switched off. Supports all Pebble models, with an optional dithered gray nose on monochrome screens. Backlight-triggered spins are also available on compatible models.

A huge thank-you to zbw / zbzbw for the lovely original analog watchface, clever spinner clock and cat artwork!
