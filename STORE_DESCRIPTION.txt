a digital variant of "loading cat" by zbw / zbzbw. really like the original, just wanted a digital clock too. thanks to zbw for the watchface and cat artwork!

stacked time on the left, optional temperature on the right. the spinner follows the seconds, and you can flick your wrist to give it a spin. a few fonts and animation options in settings. works on all pebble models.

original: https://github.com/zbzbw/loadingcat-pebble
